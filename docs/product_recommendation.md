# Product Experiment Recommendation

## Executive Summary

The treatment experience produced an application completion rate of **22.92%** compared with **20.55%** for the control group.

This represents an absolute lift of **2.37%** and a relative lift of **11.54%**.

The 95% confidence interval for the conversion difference is **1.23% to 3.51%**, with a p-value of **0.00005**.

## Segment Findings

- **referral (channel)**: 3.66% absolute conversion lift (p=0.0026).
- **mobile (platform)**: 2.40% absolute conversion lift (p=0.0011).
- **web (platform)**: 2.34% absolute conversion lift (p=0.0146).
- **search (channel)**: 2.30% absolute conversion lift (p=0.0114).

## Product Recommendation

Proceed with a broader rollout of the treatment experience while continuing to monitor conversion and segment-level performance.

Segment-level results should be monitored during rollout because the treatment may perform differently across acquisition channels and platforms.
